Configuration Options Exposed (Modular Architecture)

Glass & Track Parameters
  • tubeRadius: Outer tube diameter
  • glassBlur: Internal blur/frost level
  • glassOpacity: Translucency level (0.0 - 1.0)
  • rimGlowIntensity: Edge luminous highlight intensity
  • glassColor: Base frosted tint color
Flow Stream & Animation Parameters
  • coreColor / glowColor: Vibrant blue & cyan energy colors
  • flowSpeed: Internal highlight pulse velocity
  • direction: Forward (1) or Reverse (-1)
  • highlightDensity: Density of gradient highlights
  • progress: Timeline draw progress (0.0 - 1.0)
